Leadership

The person at the helm

Photo

Principal & Founder

Dr. Jimson
Olufuye

Dr. Jimson Olufuye is the founding principal of Kontemporary Konsulting Ltd and one of Nigeria's most recognised voices in ICT governance and digital policy. With over 30 years in the sector, he has advised government institutions and international development bodies on technology strategy, cybersecurity and digital inclusion.

He serves on several global advisory bodies and has represented Nigeria and Africa at the highest levels of international internet governance discourse — including the United Nations General Assembly.

Delivered the Maiden UNGA Plenary Speech at WSIS+20 — representing Africa's multi-stakeholder community at the United Nations General Assembly, New York.
