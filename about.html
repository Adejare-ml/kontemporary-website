Our Story

Three decades of shaping how Africa builds with technology

Kontemporary Konsulting Ltd was founded with a clear conviction: that Africa's institutions — government, enterprise, civil society — deserve ICT advice and solutions that match the best available anywhere in the world. Fit-for-purpose. Context-aware. Results-driven.

"We are a global brand of people, process and technology."

Over 30 years, we have built a track record that spans Federal Government of Nigeria mandates, World Bank-funded programmes, European Union projects and private sector engagements across West Africa. Our work has helped organisations design better ICT policies, deploy more reliable infrastructure and navigate the complex intersection of technology and governance.

Our name is intentional. The "K" that begins Kontemporary — and each of our three service areas — signals that we approach every engagement with fresh thinking, current methods and a contemporary understanding of how technology reshapes organisations and societies.

Meet the principal

Dr. Jimson Olufuye Principal & Founder

Principal & Founder

Dr. Jimson Olufuye

Dr. Jimson Olufuye built Kontemporary Konsulting into one of Nigeria's most respected ICT advisory firms over more than three decades. His expertise spans ICT policy, digital governance, cybersecurity and systems integration.

Beyond his consultancy work, he is a prominent voice in global internet governance. He has participated in IGF sessions, AfICTA forums and WSIS reviews, and serves on advisory groups at the United Nations level. He is widely recognised as a leading advocate for inclusive, multi-stakeholder digital governance across Africa.

Maiden UNGA Plenary Speaker at WSIS+20 · Member, UN Multi-Stakeholder Advisory Group 2025 · IGF Expert Group · AfICTA Representative
