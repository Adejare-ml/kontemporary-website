Practice Area 01

Komputers

Infrastructure is the foundation of every digital ambition. Kontemporary designs, procures and deploys ICT hardware and software environments that are reliable, scalable and appropriate for the Nigerian and West African context. We do not impose foreign templates — we engineer solutions that fit.

From data centres and local area networks to voice-over-IP deployments and end-user device management, our Komputers practice covers the full infrastructure lifecycle: assessment, design, supply, integration and maintenance.

ICT infrastructure design and architecture
Hardware procurement and deployment
Software integration and configuration
Data and voice communication systems
Network design, LAN/WAN and connectivity
Ongoing maintenance and support

Konsulting

Technology decisions made poorly cost organisations years and millions. Kontemporary's Konsulting practice helps government agencies, parastatals, development organisations and private sector clients make the right technology choices — backed by evidence, experience and a clear understanding of institutional context.

We have worked with the Federal Government of Nigeria on national ICT strategy, with World Bank-funded programmes on technology governance, and with EU-supported projects on digital inclusion. We bring the same level of rigour to every client, regardless of scale.

ICT strategy and policy development
Digital transformation advisory
Technology procurement advisory
Internet governance and policy
Capacity building and training
Project management and oversight

Kreativities

In a world where organisations live and compete online, digital presence and digital security are not optional. Kontemporary's Kreativities practice designs, builds and protects the digital assets of our clients — from web applications and content platforms to cybersecurity frameworks and incident response.

We have delivered cybersecurity advisory for development contexts, published research on cybersecurity for the fourth industrial revolution, and built digital solutions for public and private sector clients across Nigeria. Our approach is always security-first, always user-centred.

Web and enterprise application design
Digital content and communications
Cybersecurity strategy and advisory
Security awareness and training
Vulnerability assessment
Incident response planning
